# Anonymous learner feedback

Do not write your name or contact information.

1. Approximately how many minutes did you spend?
2. Did you complete the notebook? **Yes / Partly / No**
3. Rate installation clarity: **1 unclear – 5 very clear**
4. Rate the notebook instructions: **1 unclear – 5 very clear**
5. Rate the claim-boundary explanation: **1 unclear – 5 very clear**
6. Which task was most useful?
7. Which instruction, plot, or term was most confusing?
8. What is one thing you understand now that you did not understand before?
9. What should be removed, shortened, or added?
10. In one sentence, why can this exercise not establish a biological treatment effect?

The ratings are diagnostic feedback for revision, not a validated educational scale.
