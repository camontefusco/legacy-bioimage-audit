Use the included synthetic dataset. Short answers are sufficient.

1. What do you expect to change when the threshold or minimum object size changes?
2. Can any configuration be called biologically correct from these panels alone? Why?

## During the notebook

3. Which configuration produced the largest change in foreground area for one panel?

5. What visual feature in an overlay made you question a mask?
6. What does mask IoU compare in this exercise?

## Interpretation checkpoint

7. In one sentence, explain why mask agreement is not segmentation accuracy.
8. What is the unit of description in this exercise?
9. Name one biological claim that the exercise cannot support.
10. List two things needed before making a treatment-effect claim.
11. At what point should this analysis stop rather than proceed to hypothesis testing?

## Design the next study

13. Name one acquisition control, one metadata field, and one validation resource you would request.
