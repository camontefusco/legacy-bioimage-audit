# Claim boundary

## Supported

- The historical numerical outputs can be traced to a recovered code branch.
- The manuscript described a different segmentation configuration from the branch that generated its reported values.
- Different reasonable analysis branches produce materially different measurements on the same rendered panels.
- The available files are insufficient for treatment-effect or host-context inference.
- The package can teach reproducibility, sensitivity analysis, quality control, and experimental-unit design.

## Not supported

- Any causal or inferential statement about tobramycin, macrophages, time, bacterial aggregation, viability, clearance, persistence, tolerance, or biofilm formation.
- Segmentation accuracy or superiority without ground-truth annotation.
- Generalization beyond the 12 selected panels.
- Physical-size measurements without original calibration.

## Required wording

Every public description should call this a **historical reconstruction and educational case study based on 12 rendered thesis panels**.
