# Community conduct

Participation should be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the learning resource. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Critique methods and claims rather than people. Remember that the module is partly about learning from incomplete research records; questions and mistakes should be handled as opportunities for clearer documentation and instruction.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and interactions that violate these expectations. Conduct concerns should be reported privately to the project owner through the contact route listed on the eventual repository profile. A dedicated contact address must be added before public release.
